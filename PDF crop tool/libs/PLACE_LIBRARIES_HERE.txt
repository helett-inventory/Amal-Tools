Place these three files in this "libs" folder:

1. pdf.min.js          (PDF.js main library)
2. pdf.worker.min.js   (PDF.js worker - must sit next to pdf.min.js)
3. pdf-lib.min.js      (pdf-lib library)

Download links (open in browser, save the file, no build step needed):

- PDF.js:
  https://mozilla.github.io/pdf.js/  -> "Download" -> use build/pdf.min.js and build/pdf.worker.min.js
  or directly from a release: https://github.com/mozilla/pdf.js/releases

- pdf-lib:
  https://unpkg.com/pdf-lib/dist/pdf-lib.min.js
  (save the page as pdf-lib.min.js)

After placing the files, the folder should look like:

  PDF crop tool/
    index.html
    style.css
    script.js
    libs/
      pdf.min.js
      pdf.worker.min.js
      pdf-lib.min.js

Then just double-click index.html to run the app - no server, no install.
